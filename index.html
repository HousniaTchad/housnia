Housnia

A free, offline mobile app that helps women and girls in Chad understand their periods, follow their pregnancy and recognise danger signs after childbirth.

Try the prototype Open source code

The problem

860maternal deaths per 100,000 live births
WHO Chad, 2022
38%of births attended by skilled health personnel
UNICEF, 2022
22%of people aged 15+ can read and write
CIA World Factbook

Periods are taboo, early pregnancies are common, danger signs are recognised too late, and mobile internet is rare and expensive. Most health apps need internet and assume users can read.

Our solution

Works offline

All content stays on the phone after installation. Updates arrive in small packs when a network is available.

Listen, not only read

Texts in French and Arabic; audio and video also in Chadian Arabic, for women who cannot read.

From first period to after birth

My Periods, My Pregnancy, After Baby, Learn, and an always-visible Emergency button.

Private and validated

No account, data stays on the phone. Content validated by a Chadian medical committee.

Status

Early prototype (October 2026). Next steps: field research with women, girls and midwives in N'Djamena, medical validation of content, and a pilot with 300 users. Health content in the prototype is a draft pending medical validation.
